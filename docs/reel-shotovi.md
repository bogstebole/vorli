# Reel — shot lista (9:16, ~20s)

Struktura prati onboarding JTBD: pitanje → nada → akcija.
Overlay tekstovi su iz same aplikacije (SF Mono, monohrom — beli tekst na tamnom).

## Shot 1 — Hook (0–3s)
**Overlay:** „Gde odoše pare?"
**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Close-up macro shot, crumpled paper receipts falling in slow motion onto a dark surface, moody monochrome lighting, shallow depth of field, cinematic 9:16 vertical, high contrast black and white

## Shot 2 — Problem (3–6s)
**Overlay:** „Kraj meseca. Račun prazan. Nemaš pojma zašto."
**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Overhead dolly shot, hand scrolling endlessly through a banking app on a phone in a dim room, frustrated pause, cold desaturated tones, vertical 9:16, cinematic realism

## Shot 3 — Akcija / rešenje (6–10s)
**Overlay:** „QR kod sa fiskalnog računa — dve sekunde, i sve je unutra."
**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Slow push-in shot, hand holding phone scanning a QR code on a fiscal receipt, subtle screen glow illuminating the frame, dark minimal aesthetic, monochrome with a single soft highlight, vertical 9:16
*(preko ovoga ide pravi screen-recording skeniranja iz aplikacije)*

## Shot 4 — Proof (10–15s)
**Overlay:** „Svaki račun. Svaki artikal. Svaki dinar."
**Sadržaj:** screen-recording — lista artikala + UKUPNO sa numeric transition (onboarding strana 2), pa search po kategoriji.
**Higgsfield prompt (pozadina/tranzicija):**
> Abstract slow-motion shot, glowing monospaced numbers cascading like data rain on black background, elegant minimal motion graphics feel, vertical 9:16

## Shot 5 — Nada / CTA (15–20s)
**Overlay:** „Ono što ti ostane na kraju meseca postaje ono što želiš."
**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Cinematic reveal shot, premium headphones on a dark desk next to a phone showing a savings progress bar, warm single light source cutting through darkness, aspirational mood, vertical 9:16

**CTA kartica:** logo + „Skeniraj prvi račun" (Liquid Glass dugme).

## Napomene
- Svi generisani shotovi: 9:16, monohrom/desaturisano da prate dizajn jezik aplikacije.
- Shotovi 3 i 4 su hibrid: AI pozadina + pravi screen-recording iz simulatora.
- Muzika: minimal, perkusivna — rez na beat između shotova.
