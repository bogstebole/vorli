- Action button: circle, 28×28pt, SF Symbol inside
  - PDF: "arrow.down.circle" or "arrow.down.to.line"
  - ShoppingList: "square.and.arrow.up"
  - Disabled/loading: .opacity(0.35), .disabled(true)
